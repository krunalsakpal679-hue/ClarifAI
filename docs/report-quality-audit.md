## Honest Assessment of Known Limitations & Future Work

1. **Category Mapping on Unseen Non-Standard Headings**: Documents with non-standard section headings (e.g. "Product Warranty and Defect Remedies" in supply contracts) correctly trigger the fine-grained category (`Warranty`), which diverges from legacy test fixtures that grouped all risks under generic `Liability`.
2. **Scanned OCR Noise in Low-Resolution PDFs**: Heavily degraded scans with OCR artifacts require pre-segmentation spelling correction before heading parsing to reach 100% heading confidence on unnumbered contracts.
3. **Party-Centric Calibration on Complex Multi-Party Deeds**: While two-party commercial agreements (`Customer`/`Vendor`, `Client`/`Consultant`, `Landlord`/`Tenant`) achieve high fidelity, multi-guarantor loan syndications require fine-grained role resolution per covenant.
